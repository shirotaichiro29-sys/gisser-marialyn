## ✨ Features Included

1. **Editorial Bridal Flat-Lay Experience (Matches Reference Photo)**:
   - **Responsive Couple Background**: Fullscreen portrait of Gisser & Marialyn fitted to any device with `object-fit: cover` and soft warm linen lighting.
   - **Custom Envelope Photo Liner**: Opening the envelope reveals the couple's photo as an authentic stationery lining.
   - **Framed Card Portrait**: Gold foil circular medallion with the couple's photo embedded inside the invitation card.
   - Header with `"TOGETHER WITH THEIR FAMILIES"` and graceful script calligraphy `"Gisser & Marialyn"`.
   - Powder dusty blue envelope flanked by delicate white baby's breath, dusty blue hydrangeas, garden roses, and eucalyptus florals.
   - Pearl ivory wax seal with embossed monogram and soft glow.
   - Frosted glass `"CLICK TO OPEN"` action below the envelope.
   - Realistic 3D flap opening and letter slide-out with dynamic height scaling.
   - "Re-seal Envelope" button to fold the letter back and replay the experience anytime.

2. **Luxury Theme**:
   - **Colors**: Natural Oatmeal Canvas Linen (`#ede7de`), Powder Dusty Blue (`#8fa7bc`, `#9ab4c9`), Pearl Ivory (`#fdfdf9`), Soft Charcoal Ink (`#222b32`), and Shimmering Metallic Gold.
   - **Typography**: Google Fonts (*Alex Brush*, *Cinzel*, *Cormorant Garamond*, *Great Vibes*, *Montserrat*).

3. **Audio & Effects**:
   - **Web Audio API Harp Arpeggio**: Plays a chime/harp strum upon breaking the seal (works without any external MP3 files).
   - **Ambient Floating Gold Dust**: Canvas-rendered shimmering particles floating in the background.
   - **Gold Foil Confetti Burst**: Cascades when the invitation reveals.
   - **Sound Mute / Unmute Button**: Positioned at top-right corner.

4. **Event Essentials**:
   - Live Days/Hours/Mins/Secs countdown timer to December 22, 2026 9:00 AM.
   - Interactive RSVP modal with guest count, attendance selection, and confirmation message.
   - "Add to Calendar" button (direct Google Calendar integration with exact event time).
   - "Location" button (direct Google Maps location search).
